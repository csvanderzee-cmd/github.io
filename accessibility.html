Accessibility Statement

Everyone should be able to follow the league, find a schedule and check a bracket, including people who use a screen reader, a keyboard, magnification or other assistive technology.

Our standard

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA, which includes WCAG 2.1 AA, the level required of school districts under Title II of the Americans with Disabilities Act.

As of October 29, 2026 the site partially conforms: it meets the standard on every page tested, apart from the known limitations listed below, which we are working to fix.

Features that help

  • Display settings: the Aa button in the menu bar (or “Display settings” in the mobile menu) changes how every page looks, and remembers your choice on this device:
    • High contrast – white text on black, with links and buttons in yellow and underlined. Background images, gradients and glows are removed, and match winners are marked with a check (✓) instead of a colour.
    • Text size – 100%, 125% or 150%. Pages reflow to fit, including on a phone.
    • Readable font – switches the site to Atkinson Hyperlegible, a typeface designed by the Braille Institute for low-vision readers, and removes slanted and all-capital headings.
    If Windows’ own high-contrast mode is on, the site follows it.
  • Pause animations: the “Pause animations” box in Display settings stops every moving or auto-advancing element on the site. It starts switched on if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pause live updates: the Smash tournament brackets and the Rocket League finals brackets update on their own; a button next to the live status holds them still.
  • Skip to main content: the first thing on every page when you press Tab.
  • Keyboard access: menus, filters, dialogs and the tournament display all work with the keyboard. Escape closes menus and dialogs.
  • Screen readers: tables have captions and full column names, match results are read as sentences, and live score changes are announced without moving your place.
  • Zoom and phones: pages reflow to a phone-width screen, and to high browser zoom, without sideways scrolling.
  • Text versions: the tournament flyer has a full text version on its page and on page 2 of its PDF.

Known limitations

  • The two finals videos on the home page are captioned; they are still being reviewed for information shown only on screen, which may need audio description.
  • Content provided by other services is outside our control: the tournament registration form (Google Forms) and the video player (YouTube).

How we test

Every page is checked with automated accessibility testing (axe-core and WebAIM WAVE) at desktop and phone width, and by hand with a keyboard. New pages and documents are checked before they go up.

The full Accessibility Conformance Report lists every WCAG 2.2 Level A and AA success criterion, how the site meets it, the evidence behind each answer, and what is still being worked on.

Our most recent full check, on October 29, 2026, ran axe-core against the WCAG 2.2 AA rules on all 22 pages, with live league data loaded. It found three minor coding issues (on the home page and the two Rocket League standings pages), which were fixed the same day; a re-check found no remaining issues. Automated tools catch only part of what matters, so we also welcome feedback from people using screen readers and other assistive technology.

The display settings were added the same day. Every page was checked in high contrast, and at 150% text with the readable font on both a desktop and a phone-width screen, and re-scanned with the settings panel open.
