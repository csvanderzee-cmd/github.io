PSD All Stars take second at the LA County Cup

2nd

The Palmdale School District All Stars placed second in the Rocket League bracket at the inaugural Esports Cyber Challenge — Los Angeles County Cup, held on May 30, 2026. Eighty middle school students representing fourteen districts took part, competing in Rocket League alongside a separate cybersecurity challenge.
