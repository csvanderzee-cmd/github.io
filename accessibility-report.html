What the ratings mean

  • Supports  Every page and feature in scope meets the criterion.
  • Partially supports  Some content does not yet meet it. The remarks say exactly which.
  • Not applicable  The site has no content the criterion applies to. The remarks say why.

Scope

All 26 pages served from psdesports.com were evaluated:

  • Site: home, Hall of Champions, In the News, Approved Games, accessibility statement, this report, and the page-not-found page.
  • Rocket League: standings, schedule and player stats for both divisions (Early Release and Late Release), rules, match setup, badge guide, the finals page, and the two full-screen finals bracket displays.
  • Smash Bros tournament: overview, both division brackets (including their full-screen display view), rules, flyer, the flyer’s print source, and the organisers’ schedule planner. The last two are not linked from the site but are publicly reachable, so they were included.
  • Documents: the tournament flyer PDF, the only document the site links to.
  • Display settings: every page was evaluated in its default appearance, and again with high contrast, 150% text and the readable font.

Live content (standings, results, brackets) was evaluated as it was on the evaluation date, loaded from the league’s data. Content provided by other services is listed under third-party content and is outside this report.

How the site was tested

  1. Automated testing: axe-core 4.10.2 against every WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 A/AA rule on every page, after live data had loaded, with menus, dialogs and the display settings panel opened. Re-run after every fix. Result: no violations on any page.
  2. Colour contrast by measurement: wherever text sits on a gradient, photo or translucent panel (which automated tools cannot judge), the page was rendered, the text hidden, and every background pixel behind it compared with the text colour. The 5th-percentile (near-worst) ratio had to meet 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text.
  3. Keyboard only: every menu, filter, dialog, disclosure and the display settings panel operated with Tab, Shift+Tab, Enter, Space, the arrow keys and Escape. Focus order, focus visibility, focus containment in dialogs and focus return on close were each checked.
  4. Reflow and resize: every page at 320 CSS pixels wide (equivalent to 400% zoom on a 1280-pixel screen) and at 375 pixels with 150% text and the readable font, checked for horizontal scrolling and lost content.
  5. Text spacing: every page with WCAG 1.4.12 spacing applied (line height 1.5, paragraph spacing 2×, letter spacing 0.12em, word spacing 0.16em), checked for clipped or overlapping text.
  6. Non-text contrast: borders of every form field and option button measured against their surroundings.
  7. Code inspection: names, roles, states, live regions, language, page titles, headings and landmarks reviewed in the source.
  8. Document inspection: the PDF’s tag tree, language, title and image alternative text confirmed in the file.

Environment: Microsoft Edge (Chromium) on Windows 11, with desktop and phone-width viewports emulated.

Not yet done: testing with screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver), with Safari and Firefox, and on physical phones and tablets. The accessible names, roles and live regions those tools rely on were verified in code and by axe, but not yet heard through a screen reader. This is planned, and the report will be updated with the results.

Level A success criteria

WCAG 2.2 Level A: 31 criteria
CriterionRatingRemarks and evidence
1.1.1 Non-text ContentSupports Informative images (school badges beside names, video thumbnails, setup screenshots, the flyer) have text alternatives; badges next to a school’s written name are marked decorative so the name is not read twice. Icons and emoji in labels are hidden from screen readers. The flyer image has a full text version on its page.
1.2.1 Audio-only and Video-only (Prerecorded)Not applicable The site has no audio-only or video-only media.
1.2.2 Captions (Prerecorded)Partially supports The two championship videos on the home page (25-26 finals, embedded; 24-25 championship, linked) have reviewed captions on YouTube. The Spring Season Highlights video, also linked from the home page, has not yet been reviewed.
1.2.3 Audio Description or Media Alternative (Prerecorded)Partially supports The three videos are being reviewed for information shown only on screen (scores, on-screen text) that is not spoken, which would need audio description or a text alternative.
1.3.1 Info and RelationshipsSupports Headings, lists, landmarks and data tables use native markup; every data table has a name (a caption or label) and header cells for its columns or rows; form fields have programmatic labels; groups of related controls are fieldsets or labelled groups.
1.3.2 Meaningful SequenceSupports Reading and focus order follow the visual order on every page, including the stacked phone layouts.
1.3.3 Sensory CharacteristicsSupports No instruction depends on shape, colour, size, position or sound alone. The stats score key names both a colour and a symbol (▲ / ▼).
1.4.1 Use of ColorSupports Match winners are identifiable from the score; win and loss streaks are written (W3, L1); scores above or below a player’s average carry ▲ / ▼ as well as colour; links in text are underlined. In high contrast, winners also get a ✓.
1.4.2 Audio ControlNot applicable Nothing on the site plays sound automatically.
2.1.1 KeyboardSupports Every function works from the keyboard, including opening a school’s or player’s game log from the standings and stats (rows and cards are focusable, Enter or Space opens them), the game menus, filters, week tabs, the Smash display screen and the display settings. A scripted check found no element that responds to a pointer but cannot be focused.
2.1.2 No Keyboard TrapSupports Dialogs deliberately keep Tab inside while open; Escape and the Close button always release focus back to the control that opened them.
2.1.4 Character Key ShortcutsSupports The site has no single-character keyboard shortcuts. The Smash display screen responds to Space and the arrow keys only while it is open.
2.2.1 Timing AdjustableNot applicable Nothing on the site has a time limit. Countdowns are informational, and live updates do not require any action.
2.2.2 Pause, Stop, HideSupports “Pause animations” in Display settings stops all moving content on every page (backgrounds, tickers, pulsing indicators, rotating cards) and starts on when the device asks for reduced motion. The live brackets each have a Pause updates button.
2.3.1 Three Flashes or Below ThresholdSupports Nothing flashes. The fastest effect is a pulsing dot at under once per second.
2.4.1 Bypass BlocksSupports A “Skip to main content” link is the first focusable element on every page, and every page has a main landmark and headings.
2.4.2 Page TitledSupports Every page has a unique, descriptive title. (/smash/ forwards to the tournament overview and shares its title.)
2.4.3 Focus OrderSupports Focus moves in reading order. Opening a dialog moves focus into it, Tab and Shift+Tab stay inside it, and closing returns focus to the row or card that opened it. Tested with the keyboard on the standings and player stats dialogs; the Smash rules and display dialogs use the same pattern, confirmed in code.
2.4.4 Link Purpose (In Context)Supports Link text, or link text with its card or sentence, says where each link goes.
2.5.1 Pointer GesturesSupports No function needs a multi-finger or path-based gesture.
2.5.2 Pointer CancellationSupports All actions fire on release (standard click), so a press can be cancelled by moving away.
2.5.3 Label in NameSupports Every control’s accessible name contains its visible label, so it can be activated by voice using the words on screen (for example the “Aa” button is named “Aa, display settings”).
2.5.4 Motion ActuationNot applicable Nothing responds to shaking or tilting the device.
3.1.1 Language of PageSupports Every page declares English (lang="en"), as does the PDF.
3.2.1 On FocusSupports Focusing an element never changes the page or moves focus. Game menus show their links on focus, which is not a change of context.
3.2.2 On InputSupports Changing a filter, sort or setting updates the results in place; nothing navigates or submits on its own.
3.2.6 Consistent HelpSupports The Accessibility link, which leads to the contact for help, is in the same place at the foot of every page that scrolls. (The two full-screen finals displays, sized for a stream, have no footer.)
3.3.1 Error IdentificationNot applicable No input on the site is validated or submitted. Team registration uses Google Forms (third party).
3.3.2 Labels or InstructionsSupports Every search box, filter, setting and planner field has a visible label.
3.3.7 Redundant EntryNot applicable There are no multi-step processes that ask for information.
4.1.2 Name, Role, ValueSupports Custom controls expose their role, name and state: menu buttons report expanded or collapsed, week tabs and filters report which is selected (aria-pressed), dialogs are labelled and modal, clickable rows and cards are buttons with descriptive names. Every aria-label sits on an element whose role allows one. Verified by axe and in code; screen reader confirmation is pending.

WCAG 2.2 removed 4.1.1 Parsing, which is therefore not listed.

Document: tournament flyer PDF

/smash/PSD-Smash-Tournament-Flyer.pdf is a tagged PDF: it has a tag tree, its language set to English, a document title, a heading, and alternative text on its image, and page 2 repeats the flyer as plain text. This was confirmed by inspecting the file; it has not yet been run through a dedicated PDF checker such as PAC 2024, which is planned.

Third-party content

These services are linked from or embedded in the site but are provided by others. They are outside this report, and the site does not rely on them for any information that is not also available on its own pages:

  • Google Forms: the tournament registration form.
  • YouTube: the embedded video player and the linked videos (the captions on our videos are covered above).
  • Google Sheets: where league data is kept. Visitors never see it; the site renders the data as its own pages.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: visit counting, with no visible interface.

Known limitations and plan

  • Videos (1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.2.5): review the Spring Season Highlights captions, and check all three videos for on-screen information that is not spoken. Where there is any, add audio description or a text summary beside the video.
  • Assistive technology testing: confirm the main tasks (find a school’s standing, check a schedule, follow a bracket, change display settings) with NVDA and VoiceOver, and in Safari and Firefox.
  • PDF: run the flyer through PAC 2024.

If you find a barrier this report does not mention, please email csvanderzee@palmdalesd.org with the page address and what you were trying to do.

Fixed during this evaluation

Each of these was found during the evaluation, fixed the same day, and re-tested.

  • Contrast (1.4.3): the division schedule lines on the home page’s league cards (over a photo); the win and loss streak badges on both standings pages; the Home and Away tags and the selected week tab on both schedules; and the division label at the top of every Rocket League page (over the hero photo).
  • Field borders (1.4.11): the search box and filters on both player stats pages, the schedule planner’s fields, and the option buttons in Display settings.
  • Use of colour (1.4.1): scores above or below a player’s average now show ▲ or ▼ as well as green or red.
  • Focus order (2.4.3): the player game-log dialog on both stats pages now keeps Tab inside it while it is open.
  • Label in name (2.5.3): the “Aa” display settings button’s name now includes “Aa”.
  • Name, role, value (4.1.2): several elements carried an aria-label that screen readers may ignore without a role (home-page countdown and photo strip, game grade lists, streak badges, player-of-the-week banner); each now has an appropriate role. The Early Release streak badges also gained the spoken “3 win streak” wording the Late Release page already had. The week tabs on both schedules now report which week is selected.
  • Reflow (1.4.10): the coach email addresses on the home page now wrap on a 320-pixel screen.
  • Earlier the same day: the home page’s school links, a skipped heading level, and the standings pages’ footer landmark (from the automated audit described on the accessibility statement).
  • Tables (1.3.1): the match settings table now marks each setting’s name as its row header, and the two division tables on In the News are named by their headings.
  • Dialogs (1.3.1, 2.1.1): the school and player dialogs on the standings and stats pages now title themselves at the right heading level, and their scrolling game logs can be reached and scrolled with the keyboard.
  • States shown only in some weeks (4.1.2): the finals bracket’s winner crown and game scores, the schedule’s bye-week card, the dialogs’ win/loss/bye pills, and the home page’s pre-season countdown now have roles, so their spoken labels are read.
  • Consistent identification (3.2.4): the Smash bracket pages’ “Pause live updates” button now reads “Pause updates”, the same as everywhere else.
