Coach's Guide

Set Up a Match

How to create or join a private Rocket League match on match day, with the settings every league match uses. The Home team creates the match; the Away team joins it.

psdesports.com/rocket-league/match-setup.html

Home team

Creates the match and sends the name and password to the opposing coach. Steps 1–7.

Away team

Joins with the name and password from the home coach. Steps 1–2, then 6–7.

  1. 1
    Both teams

    Open Private Match

    From the main screen, select Play, then Private Match.

  2. 2
    Both teams

    Create or Join

    Home Choose Create Private Match. You can do this on your own account or have a student on your team set it up.

    Away Choose Join Private Match and enter the name and password the home coach sent you, then skip to step 6.

  3. 3
    Home team

    Use the League Settings

    Set the match up exactly like this. Then open Team Settings, type in both schools' names and, if you like, pick colors.

    Private match settings for league matches

    ArenaDFH Stadium

    Match AdminDisabled
  4. 4
    Home team

    Reset the Mutators

    Open Mutator Settings and press Restore Defaults, so nothing left over from a previous game carries into the match.

  5. 5
    Home team

    Create the Match & Share It

    Click Create Match and choose a fairly easy name and password. Give them to everyone on your team, then send them to the opposing coach on Google Chat.

    Need to find the other coach? Use the PSD eSports Coaches contact list (opens in a new tab).

  6. 6
    Both teams

    Wait for All 6 Players

    On the Choose Team screen, don't join a team until all 6 players are in the match. If anyone joins a team early, the game starts before everyone is in.

    Once all six are in, everyone joins their own school's team and the match begins.

  7. 7
    Both teams

    Photograph the Results

    When the match ends, keep every student in the game until you've taken a picture of the results screen.

    Both coaches then report each game's final stats to the League Administrators on Google Classroom (rule 4.4).

Keep a Copy by the Computer

Prints as black on white with the screenshots. Choose Save as PDF in the print dialog to keep a digital copy.
