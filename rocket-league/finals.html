How the Finals Work

The top 4 schools in each division play semifinals, then the Championship Final and a third-place match. Each school brings its BLUE and ORANGE teams, just like the regular season.

  1. Best of 5

    Every match is a best of 5. The first team to win 3 games wins the match.

  2. Blue and Orange both play

    In each series, BLUE plays BLUE and ORANGE plays ORANGE. A school that wins both matches wins the series.

  3. Tiebreaker game

    If the series is split 1–1, one tiebreaker game decides it. Each school picks its best team of 3 from all of its players and subs.

  4. Rosters are locked

    Players stay on the team they start with for the whole day. Subs are allowed, but once a sub plays for a team they are locked to that team. The tiebreaker game is the only exception.

  5. Two stages

    The semifinals share the arena and the bus. All BLUE teams play first: Semifinal 1 in the arena, Semifinal 2 on the bus. Then the ORANGE teams swap: Semifinal 2 in the arena, Semifinal 1 on the bus. A school's two teams never play at the same time.

  6. Final and third place

    A semifinal tiebreaker stays where that semifinal's ORANGE match was played, so nobody changes stage for one game: both tiebreakers are played at the same time, Semifinal 2 in the arena and Semifinal 1 on the bus. The Championship Final is played entirely in the arena, BLUE then ORANGE, while the two semifinal losers play for third place on the bus.

  7. Settings, not sign-ins

    Players do not sign in to personal or school accounts at the tournament. Each player gets a couple of minutes before a match to change settings. Coaches: practice resetting all keybinds and putting in each player's own binds, so it takes seconds on the day.

Full details are in Section 7 of the rules.
