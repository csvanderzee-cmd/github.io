Group Stage

Round robin · Win = 3 pts · Top 2 advance

Elimination Bracket

Single elimination · Best of 3

How Ties Are Broken

  1. Match points (Win = 3, Loss = 0)
  2. Head-to-head result between the tied teams
  3. Remaining-stock differential — stock the team had left in the games it won, minus stock its opponents had left in the games they won
  4. Games won across the group

Individual games never end in a draw: an equal-stock, equal-damage finish goes to the game's built-in Sudden Death, so every match finishes 2–0 or 2–1.
