Group Stage

Elimination Bracket

Single elimination · Best of 3

How Ties Are Broken

  1. Match points (Win = 3, Loss = 0)
  2. Head-to-head result between the tied teams
  3. Remaining-stock differential — every stock this team had left across the group, minus every stock its opponents had left
  4. Games won across the group

No game ends in a draw. If the timer runs out, the team with more stock left wins; if stock is level the console goes to Sudden Death, so every match finishes 2–0 or 2–1.
