Flyer text

Super Smash Bros. Tournament

PSD Esports · Palmdale School District 2026–27.
2v2 Doubles · One Day · Two Divisions.

Important: every school brings an adult referee

Stations run simultaneously, so each school must provide at least one adult to referee for their division’s full schedule. Referees are assigned by organizers and briefed on the day.

When and where

Saturday, November 14 · 8:00 AM start · Quail Valley Esports Arena.

Divisions

  • Grade 5 & Under
  • Grades 6–8

Who can enter

Teams of 2 players plus an optional locked alternate. Schools may enter up to two teams (e.g., Shadow Hills Red / Gold) playing only within their division.

How the day runs

Round-robin group stage (3 pts/win, top 2 advance to single-elimination bracket). Matches are best of 3 with no draws.

What to bring

Nothing but players. Consoles, screens, and Joy-Con/Pro controllers are provided (no personal controllers for match play; personal Switches allowed for warm-up only).

Rules

  • 2v2 Doubles
  • Best of 3
  • 3 Stock
  • 6 Minutes
  • Battlefield Only
  • Hazards Off
  • Items Off
  • Stock Sharing On
  • Base Roster (No Miis)
  • Team Damage On

Register by Friday, Oct 30

Entry form: forms.gle/PHLTBQBWvUJktBgw8
Live bracket: psdesports.com/smash
