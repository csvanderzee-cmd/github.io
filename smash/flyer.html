Who Can Enter

  • A team is two players, plus an optional alternate. An alternate is locked to that team.
  • Schools may enter up to two teams, told apart by a colour — e.g. Shadow Hills Red and Shadow Hills Gold.
  • Teams play only within their own division.

How the Day Runs

  • Group stage: groups of three or four, round robin. A win is 3 points.
  • Top two from every group go through to a single-elimination bracket.
  • Every match is best of three. No match ends in a draw.

What to Bring

  • Nothing but your players. Consoles, controllers and screens are provided.
  • Any provided Joy-Con or Pro Controller, in any configuration. No personal controllers for match play.
  • Teams may bring their own Switch for warm-up only.

Every School Brings an Adult

Stations run simultaneously, so the tournament cannot operate without an adult at every station. Each participating school must provide at least one adult to referee for their division’s portion of the day — and to stay for that division’s full schedule, not only while their own teams are still in. Referees are assigned by the organisers and briefed on the day; wherever possible, nobody referees their own school’s matches.

Follow it live. Group tables and the elimination bracket update by themselves as results come in — on any phone, from anywhere, no refreshing. psdesports.com/smash
