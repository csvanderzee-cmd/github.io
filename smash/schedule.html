Referee notes

Record each team's remaining stock for every game, on that team's own row. Leave a game blank if it was not played.

How to record a game
How the game endedRecordExample
A team lost all its stockWinner's remaining stock, loser 04 and 0
Timer ran out, different stockBoth teams' actual stock4 and 3
Timer ran out, same stock (Sudden Death)Winner 1, loser 01 and 0
A team forfeits the gameWinner 0, forfeiting team F0 and F
  • Settings: Battlefield only, hazards off, items off, pausing off, stock sharing on, team damage on, base roster with no Mii Fighters. Wrong settings mean an immediate restart of that game.
  • Match: first team to win 2 games wins. Players may switch characters between games. Coaching is allowed between games, not during them.
  • Late or missing team: after 5 minutes, forfeit the first game. After 10 minutes, forfeit the match.
  • Disconnect or crash: before the first stock is lost, you may order a replay. After that, play continues at your discretion.
  • Decisions: referee decisions are final. Stay for your division's full schedule, even if your school's teams are out.
