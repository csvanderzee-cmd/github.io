Referee quick rules

  • Match: best of 3. First team to win 2 games wins. Leave Game 3 blank if not played.
  • Lost all stock: write the winner's stock left, loser 0.
  • Timer ran out, different stock: write both teams' actual stock.
  • Timer ran out, same stock (Sudden Death): winner 1, loser 0.
  • Forfeit: winner 0, forfeiting team F.
  • Settings: 2v2, 3 stock, 6 minutes, Battlefield only, hazards and items off. Wrong settings: restart that game.
  • Late team: 5 minutes, forfeit Game 1. 10 minutes, forfeit the match.
  • Disconnect or crash: before the first stock is lost, you may order a replay. After that, your call.
  • Coaching: between games only. Referee decisions are final.
