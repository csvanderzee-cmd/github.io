LA County Cup · May 30, 2026

PSD All Stars take second at the LA County Cup

The Palmdale School District All Stars placed second in the Rocket League bracket at the inaugural Esports Cyber Challenge, hosted by the Los Angeles County Office of Education. Eighty middle school students from fourteen districts competed; Stauffer Middle School of Downey Unified took first, and Davis Middle School of Compton Unified took third.

Match Format

How a Match Day Works

01

Six Players, Two Teams

Each school fields three players on ORANGE and three on BLUE. A player sticks with one color for the whole series.

02

Check In

Coaches confirm readiness 15 minutes before the match over Gmail Chat. The home team creates the lobby.

03

Best of Three

Both series run at the same time on DFH Stadium. A third game only happens if the series is tied 1-1.

04

Report Scores

Both coaches post final stats to Google Classroom. Combined ORANGE and BLUE records set the standings.
