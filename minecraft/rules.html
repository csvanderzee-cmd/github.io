Minecraft Capture the Flag

Rules

Match settings, scoring and how a winner is decided.

OverviewRulesHow to PlayScheduleStandingsLive Events

1. Match settings

  • Map: Capture the Flag version 1.9.5. Every match uses the same version, supplied by the league.
  • Match length: 20 minutes, the map’s full match. The host does not shorten it.
  • Team size: 5v5, five players a side. The host sets it before the match starts.
  • The host sets length and team size before starting the match and does not change them during it, except to pause.

2. Scoring

  • Flag capture: take the other team's flag from their base and bring it to your flag base: 1 point.
  • Ravager horn: the Ravager feeding ground in the forest spawns once per match at minute 9. Taking down the Ravager drops one or two horns; each horn carried back to your base scores 1 point. A carrier who is taken down drops the horn, and either team can pick it up.
  • Frags: every enemy you defeat counts on the Frags board.

3. Winning a match

  • The team with the higher Score when the timer reaches zero wins. To be decided
  • If the Score is tied: To be decided (likely the team with more Frags).
  • If both are tied: To be decided

4. Results

  • When the timer reaches zero, the host takes a screenshot of the Score board and the Frags board before anyone leaves the world.
  • Those screenshots are the official result. Both coaches confirm the numbers before the result is entered.
  • Disputes go to the league administrator with the screenshots, the same day.

5. The map: what to know

  • No front door. Each team gets doors and levers in a chest and decides where to build them, or not to build them at all.
  • No building inside either base. The whole castle is protected, not just the flag.
  • Food matters. Hunger drains quickly. Protect your food; raiding the other team's is fair play.
  • Pillager Outpost. A neutral objective; clearing it gives a loot chest with items that trade for emeralds.
  • Archer turrets and crafting better weapons as you explore are part of the game.

6. Rosters and teams

  • Schools may enter more than one team. A player is on one team for the season.
  • Subs: To be decided
  • Late roster changes: To be decided

7. Forfeits and rescheduling

  • A team that does not show within To be decided minutes of the start time forfeits.
  • Rescheduling: To be decided

8. Conduct

  • Players do not sign in to personal accounts; they use their school's Minecraft Education account.
  • No exploiting bugs, no outside programs, no griefing outside the rules of the map.
  • Respect the other team in chat and out loud. Humble in victory, gracious in defeat.
