What it is

Two teams, two castles, two flags. Grab the other team's flag, carry it home, and defend your own. Teams also score by taking down the Ravager that appears mid-match and carrying its horn back to base. Communication and strategy win matches more than fast hands.

How the season works

  1. Weekly matches, from your school. Each week your team plays one match against another team. One school hosts the world; the other joins with the join code. No travel.
  2. Results go in the same day. The host takes screenshots of the scoreboards when the timer hits zero, and the result is entered in the league sheet. Standings update on the site.
  3. Showcase and finals, live. Some matches are played live at the Quail Valley Esports Arena with casters and a stream. See Live Events. Finals format: To be decided

Divisions

  • Early Release: Minecraft Capture the Flag.
  • Late Release: To be decided (may play a different game).

Teams

  • Schools may enter more than one team.
  • Schools from outside the district are welcome. Their IT team may need to switch on cross-organization play first (see How to Play).
  • 5v5: five players a side.
