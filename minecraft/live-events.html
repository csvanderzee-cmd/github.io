Showcase and finals: the live setup

Regular-season matches are played from each school with no casters. Showcase matches and the finals are played live at the Quail Valley Esports Arena and streamed on the LevelUpPSD YouTube channel. Dates: To be decided

Rooms

  • Blue team in the arena, Red team on the bus. Each team talks freely without the other hearing its plans.
  • Casters in the arena on headsets, away from the player stations. Players wear headphones with game audio up. If the casters can't stay quiet enough, they move to a separate room.

Computers

  • Host: runs the world and the host commands. Plugged in; nobody plays on it.
  • Spectator 1 and 2: join in spectator mode and follow the action. The stream and the casters use these views.
  • Stream: spectator views, scoreboard overlay and caster mics.
